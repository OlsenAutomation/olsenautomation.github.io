This does not grant access. It tells us what exists so we can send the correct official invitation instructions later.

On your Google Business Profile: More → Business Profile settings → People and access → Add. Invite the operations email as a Manager, not an Owner.

Good questions.

The goal is careful automation with a clear evidence trail—not a promise of guaranteed rankings.

Will this guarantee that AI recommends my business?

No. Answer engines change and may personalize results. The service measures what appears, improves the public signals within your control, and reports changes honestly.

Do you need my passwords?

No. Never send passwords or codes. When access is needed, you retain ownership and invite an Olsen Automation account with the smallest practical role.

What happens after I send the file?

Olsen Automation validates the intake, confirms any missing ownership steps, creates the local monitor, performs a manual evidence run, and schedules recurring collection and due-date reporting.

What can run without me?

Monitoring and reports can run unattended. Public edits can run only inside your standing permissions. Pricing, identity changes, deletions, paid services, and unapproved client content always pause.
