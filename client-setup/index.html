    One step at a time.
    Your Wix account stays yours.

    This page guides you. It does not send invitations or connect to Wix.

    Follow the clicks.

    These cropped screenshots show the Wix dashboard, rather than the website editor. Your dashboard may look slightly different.

    1 Open Settings

    From the dashboard of the website you own, choose Settings in the left menu.

    Tap the image to enlarge.

    2 Open Roles & permissions

    Under General, choose Roles & permissions.

    Tap the image to enlarge.

    3 Invite Collaborators

    Choose Invite Collaborators.

    Tap the image to enlarge.

    4 Enter Brian’s email

    Paste brian@olsenautomation.com into the email field.

    Tap the image to enlarge.

    5 Select Website Manager

    Select Website Manager. Leave Admin (Co-Owner) unselected.

    Tap the image to enlarge.

    6 Send Invite

    After entering the email and choosing the role, select Send Invite. The example button is disabled because its email field is empty.

    Tap the image to enlarge.
